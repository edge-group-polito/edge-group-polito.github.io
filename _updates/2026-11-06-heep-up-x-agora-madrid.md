Hello X-HEEPers! 💃 We are happy to invite you to a new edition of the X-AGORA, the X-HEEP
eXchange space, taking place alongside HEEP-UP on November 12–13, 2026, in Madrid, Spain!

📈 Since its first commit, the X-HEEP Platform has grown into an international open-source
hardware project, bringing together researchers and developers from academia and industry.
Now supported by a consortium between EPFL, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, and
Politecnico di Torino, X-HEEP continues to grow through the contributions of its community.

**November 12, 09:00–18:00 | HEEP-UP** (how to go from an idea to a full RISC-V SoC)
A day for newcomers who want to learn about X-HEEP and its ecosystem, and for those trying
to KEEP-UP with the latest improvements on the platform: introductory talks and hands-on
tutorials. Registration is required for the hands-on sessions, and places are limited!

**November 13, 09:00–12:00 | 5th X-AGORA** (the X-HEEP eXchange space)
Our traditional community gathering, where everyone working with X-HEEP is invited to share
projects, experiences, and latest developments. One slide, up to two minutes, any topic
related to X-HEEP! The session concludes with presentations of the latest X-HEEP-based chip
tape-outs (CHEEPs).

Want to present? Add your slide to the shared presentation by November 12, respecting the
provided template: one slide per topic or project, two minutes at most.

🗓️ When: November 12–13, 2026
📍 Where: ETS de Ingenieros Industriales, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, Spain
💻 Online participation: https://lnkd.in/dvy5ya2d

Everyone is welcome, and there is no need to register for the talks. Forward this invitation
to colleagues and students who might be interested!
